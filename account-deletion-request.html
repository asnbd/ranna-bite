RannaBite Account Deletion Request

At RannaBite, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your RannaBite account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

Important Considerations:
  • Irreversible Action: Once your account and data are deleted, this action is permanent and cannot be undone. All associated personal information, purchased Recipe Credits, and unlocked Premium Content will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
  • Processing Time: The deletion process involves verification and manual steps by our team to ensure the security of your request. Please note that account and data deletion requests may take several business days to complete.

How to Request Account Deletion:

The easiest and most direct way to request account and data deletion is through the RannaBite app itself:

  1. Open the RannaBite app on your mobile device.
  2. Ensure you are logged in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Navigate to the "Settings" page.
  4. Select the "Account Deletion Request" option under "Account" section.
  5. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your request.

If you cannot access the in-app option:

If you are unable to access the RannaBite app or the in-app account deletion option (e.g., due to a lost device, app uninstallation, or technical issue), you may submit a manual request by sending us an email from the same email address you used to create your account.

Please send your request to our support team at the email address below. To help us process your request efficiently and securely, please use the provided email template.
