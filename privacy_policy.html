Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-06-26

RannaBite ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use our mobile application ("the app"), how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you register or log in to RannaBite using Firebase Authentication, we collect certain basic account information:

  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Your display name and profile picture (if using Google Sign-In)
This data is used strictly for authentication and managing your access to your personalized data (such as recipe credits and unlocked content).

b. Virtual Currency ("Recipe Credits")

RannaBite uses a virtual currency system called recipe credits. These are non-transferable, non-refundable units that can be used to unlock premium recipe content in the app. Recipe credits:

  • Are not real currency or legal tender.
  • Cannot be converted into real money or transferred to other users.
  • Are tied to your unique Firebase user account (UID).
  • Are stored securely in our Firebase Firestore database.
We track credits purchased, spent, and remaining for your account, but we do not track how you use any unlocked content beyond access control.

c. Payment Information

In-app purchases (e.g., buying recipe credits) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, access, or store any payment card details or personal billing information.

d. Usage & Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics, including:

  • Screens viewed
  • Feature usage (e.g., recipe unlocks)
  • App session durations
This data helps us improve the app experience. No personally identifiable data is used in analytics, and you may opt-out through your device settings.

e. Crash & Error Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect and analyze app crashes. Crash reports may include:

  • Device information (e.g., model, OS version)
  • Crash logs and events leading to the crash
These reports are used to fix bugs and improve performance. They do not include names, emails, or personal content.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and identify users
  • To manage virtual currency (recipe credits)
  • To sync and store user-specific data (unlocked recipes, credit balance)
  • To provide support and resolve issues
  • To analyze usage trends and improve app features
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored in Firebase services (Firestore and Authentication). We use Firebase's built-in security and encryption mechanisms to protect your data. Your personalized data is linked to your Firebase UID and cannot be accessed by other users.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: for secure user login and identity management.
  • Firebase Firestore: for storing user data like credits and unlocked recipes.
  • Firebase Analytics: for anonymized usage tracking and performance insights.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: for app crash/error reporting.
  • Google Play Billing: for handling in-app purchases.
  • Google Sign-In: for login via Google account (name, email, profile image).
These services may collect data as required to perform their functions, governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account-related data (email, UID, credit balance, unlocked recipes) for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, this data will be deleted from our systems. Crash and analytics data is retained according to Firebase default retention settings.

6. User Control & Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent or stop using the app at any time
To make a request, contact us at the support email listed below.

7. Children's Privacy

RannaBite is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. Any updates will be posted in-app or on this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
