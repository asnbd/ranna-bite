Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-06-26

At RannaBite, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using RannaBite, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

We use Firebase Authentication to allow users to sign up or log in using:

  • Email and password
  • Google account
When you sign up, we collect basic account identifiers such as your email address and a unique user ID (UID) generated by Firebase. We do not collect your name, phone number, or other personal details.

b. In-App Purchases

We use Google Play Billing for in-app purchases (e.g., buying recipe credits). We do not collect or store payment details. Purchases are processed securely through Google Play.

c. User Data

Your recipe credits and unlocked premium recipes are stored securely in Firebase Firestore, linked to your user ID. We do not access, collect, or store any other personal content (e.g., names, profile info, etc.).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your account and manage access
  • To track and manage your in-app purchases and recipe credits
  • To save your unlocked content tied to your account across devices

3. Data Storage and Security

We store user-related data (credits and unlocked recipes) securely in Firebase Firestore.
Firebase services are compliant with industry security standards.
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

4. Analytics and Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymized crash and performance data. This data helps us improve app stability and fix bugs. It does not contain personally identifiable information.

5. Third-Party Services

RannaBite may contain links to third-party services (e.g., Google login, Google Play). These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies before interacting with them.

6. Children's Privacy

RannaBite is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices. Any updates will be posted in-app or on this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
